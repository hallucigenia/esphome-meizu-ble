# Changelog

## 0.1.0

Initial experimental standalone release.

- ESPHome BLE polling with sample freshness and exponential failure backoff.
- Infrared sending confirmed by the puck's cache or upload acknowledgement.
- Cancellable ET4007 learning and independent C++/Python conversion checks.
- Optional HA 1.5.0 adapter with learning wizard, saved commands and migration.
- Real HA event/service checks, sanitizer tests and three ESPHome build variants.

Basic hardware validation covers one R16. Other models, long air-conditioner codes
and long-term reliability remain unverified.
